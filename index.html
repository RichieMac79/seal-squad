SEAL SQUAD

Gulf of St. Lawrence · March 1982

The protest ship is stuck in the ice, but its confrontation team is over the side with cans of green dye, heading for the seal pups. One guy from New York, in a hockey vest and armed with a hockey stick, has other ideas.

Hop between the ice floes and bonk the protesters before they spray the seals. Keep an eye on the pups too: they get spooked and flop for the water. Stand next to one to calm it down.
